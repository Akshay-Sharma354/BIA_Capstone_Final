# Store Policies

## 1. Shipping Policy
- Free standard shipping above ₹999
- Flat ₹79 fee below ₹999
- Standard delivery: 3–6 business days

## 2. Returns & Refunds Policy
- Most products are returnable within 7 days of delivery
- Items must be unused and returned with accessories and packaging
- Refunds are processed after quality check (3–7 business days)

## 3. Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations allowed until shipment
- After shipment, cancellation is not supported

## 4. Warranty Policy
- Most electronics include manufacturer warranty of 6–24 months
- Warranty claims require invoice and product identifiers

## 5. Payment & Security
- Secure payments via PCI-DSS compliant gateways
- Methods: cards, net banking, UPI, wallets, COD
- Failed payment reversals typically occur within 3–7 business days
