# Customer FAQs

## 1. Orders & Tracking

### Q1.1: How do I place an order?
Browse products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout.

### Q1.2: How can I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, we send you an email and SMS with a tracking link.

### Q1.3: What are the different order statuses?
Common statuses include "Processing", "Packed", "Shipped", "Out for Delivery", "Delivered", "Cancelled", and "Returned".

## 2. Shipping & Delivery

### Q2.1: What are the shipping charges?
Standard shipping is free for orders above ₹999. For orders below this value, a flat shipping fee of ₹79 is applied.

### Q2.2: How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery typically takes 3–6 business days depending on your location.

### Q2.3: Do you deliver to all locations?
We deliver to most pin codes across India.

## 3. Returns & Refunds

### Q3.1: What is your return policy?
Most items are eligible for return within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in original condition.

### Q3.2: How do I initiate a return?
Go to "My Orders", select the relevant order, and click "Return/Replace".

### Q3.3: When will I receive my refund?
Once the returned item passes quality checks, refunds are processed within 3–7 business days.

## 4. Payments & Security

### Q4.1: What payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit cards, debit cards, net banking, UPI, popular wallets, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

### Q4.2: Is it safe to pay online?
Yes. We use PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways and encryption.

## 5. Support

### Q5.1: How can I contact customer support?
Email: support@example.com or use chat on website.
